METHODOLOGY & EXECUTION

Our Proven Data Process

A structured, multi-stage methodology designed for accuracy, scalability, and repeatable data quality

From Raw Data to Operational Excellence

We follow an ISO-aligned 5-stage transformation lifecycle to turn unstructured, duplicate records into standardized, governed assets.

Phase 01

1. Discovery & Data Audit

We analyze your legacy data repositories, identify record duplication levels, missing attributes, and align on project scope, rules, and taxonomies (e.g. UNSPSC, eCl@ss, custom schemas).

  • Legacy database extraction & profiling
  • Duplication rate estimation
  • Dictionary & taxonomy alignment
Phase 02

2. Standardization & Normalization

Applying automated algorithms to standardize units of measure (UOM), manufacturer names, acronyms, and character casing across all records.

  • UOM standardization
  • Manufacturer & supplier name cleanup
  • Noun-Modifier catalog structuring
Phase 03

3. Attribute Extraction & Enrichment

Our domain specialists extract critical technical attributes from datasheets, drawings, and supplier catalogs to complete sparse material records.

  • Automated & manual attribute extraction
  • PDF datasheet mining
  • Description generation (Short/Long descriptions)
Phase 04

4. Multi-Tier Quality Audit

Every cleansed batch passes through a 3-tier quality control protocol (automated validation rules + senior domain engineer audit + sampling reviews).

  • Automated schema validation
  • Domain specialist sanity check
  • 99%+ compliance milestone sign-off
Phase 05

5. Integration & Governance Handoff

Delivering production-ready records formatted for your ERP/CMMS system (SAP, Oracle, Maximo) along with data governance guidelines.

  • ERP/CMMS system loading files
  • Stewardship workflow setup
  • Data governance training & documentation
