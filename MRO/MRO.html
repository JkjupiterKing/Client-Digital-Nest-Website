MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul)


MRO refers to the activities and processes required to maintain, repair, and overhaul assets, such as equipment, machinery, vehicles, and facilities. These activities are crucial for industries such as aviation, manufacturing, energy, and transportation, where regular maintenance is essential to ensure operational efficiency and safety. MRO processes usually require extensive inventory management, as businesses need to keep track of spare parts, tools, and other components for maintenance activities.
